1. Information We Collect

Growth collects and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Child's basic information (name, birth date, gender)

  • Profile pictures (stored locally on your device)

All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods. We do not:

  • Upload your data to any servers

  • Access your data without your permission

3. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • Local data encryption
  • Secure storage methods
  • No cloud synchronization

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Stop using the app and delete all data
